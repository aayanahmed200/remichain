How a match is chosen

Four weighted factors, applied to every donation–request pair. No manual triage.

01

Urgency decides first

A critical request outranks a routine one before any other factor is considered.

02

Expiry breaks the tie

Stock expiring within a fortnight is offered before stock that can wait months.

03

Distance and quantity settle it

Great-circle distance between facilities, then how much of the need the donation covers.
