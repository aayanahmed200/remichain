How it works

Three steps from shelf to patient

No spreadsheets, no phone chain. Post the surplus, post the need, and let the scoring engine do the introductions.

01

Donate

Log surplus medicine, PPE or equipment with a quantity and an expiry date, so nothing sits forgotten in a stockroom.

02

Match

Every open request is scored against available stock on urgency, expiry, distance and quantity fit. The best pairing wins.

03

Deliver

The match is proposed with an exact quantity, stock is reserved, and both facilities see it on the shared dashboard.

Why it matters

Every handoff keeps usable care out of the waste bin.

Expired stock is a double loss: the money spent to buy it, and the treatment a patient never received. RemiChain closes that gap by making the nearest good match the easiest one to act on.

  • Four scoring factors: urgency, expiry, proximity, quantity fit
  • Stock is reserved the moment a match is proposed — no double promises
  • Partial matches keep the remainder available for another facility
  • Open JSON API for inventory, requests and matches
